Overall Look and Feel of Aqualens
===============================
The AquaLens website captures the calmness and vibrancy of safely exploring the ocean 
through a blue-based colour palette with bright accents and a clean, simple design 
featuring soft, rounded edges.

Colours
========
Our colour palette will consist mostly of blue to mimic the ocean and convey a sense of 
reliability and security according to colour theory. We’ll also use yellow accents, as 
it’s blue’s complementary colour and mimics sunshine and happiness.
- #004E7A
- #B9D5E7
- #FCC107
- #87A0B2
- #A4BEF3
- #FFFFFF
- #000000


Iconography
===========
Our icons will be clean and simple to contrast with our fun and vibrant backgrounds, 
ensuring clarity and balance throughout the site.

Typography
==========
We will be using Arial and Sans Serif from Google Fonts for their clean, 
legible appearance. Their simplicity reflects clarity and professionalism while 
keeping our text easy to read across all devices.

Links
=====
Links should clearly change colour when hovered over or clicked to show interaction. 
This detail helps users understand what elements are clickable while keeping the design 
clean and consistent.

Buttons
=======
Buttons will have rounded edges and change shade when hovered over to signal interactivity. 
Their smooth transitions and simple shape reflect the calm, approachable tone of the site.

Layout and Spacing
==================
Our layout will have generous spacing to keep content clear and organized. Each page 
will have a slightly different structure to keep users engaged, while remaining fully 
responsive across all device sizes.

Accesibility
============
Our website will use clear fonts and easy-to-read layouts to keep text legible 
against our backgrounds. We’ll also include alt text for our images and keyboard navigation.
